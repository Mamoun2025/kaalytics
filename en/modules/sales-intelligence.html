The sales pipeline losing 15–20% in efficiency

Between 2022 and 2024, B2B conversion rates declined by 15 to 20 percent. This decline reflects a reality: sales reps spend more time manually qualifying and less time selling. According to 2024 benchmarks, average B2B new business conversion remains at 15–25 percent, but skill gaps are enormous.

The real opportunity: High-intent leads (demo requests) convert at 75–80 percent. Low-intent leads convert at 5–10 percent. No company optimizes this priority ratio with precision.

Source: Lunas Consulting — B2B Sales Benchmarks 2026

Sales Intelligence solves this with a single key metric: prospect scoring. Each lead receives a 0–100 score based on real potential, not intuition. Your team focuses on the 20 percent generating 80 percent of revenue.

Your sales team's algorithm

Your ERP contains raw data. Sales Intelligence transforms it into actionable intelligence. Three data sources cross-referenced automatically:

Purchase history

Frequency, volume, categories, growth/decline trend. The system recognizes patterns: "this customer always buys the same week of the month" or "their volume grows 15 percent every January."

Equipment fleet

Equipment in service, brands, years, predictive maintenance. Lets you suggest compatible parts the customer will need to order in 2–3 months (before they even know).

Cross-sell potential

Compatible products, estimated opportunity size, optimal timing calculated. Example: "this customer hasn't bought replacement parts in 8 months → predictive maintenance suggests intervention due."

Daily result: Every morning, your rep sees the 10 priority prospects with 0–100 scores, the reason for the ranking, and the optimal message to send. Zero time manually qualifying. Zero guesswork—everything calculated. Sales decisions become reproducible and scalable: what works for one rep automatically replicates across the entire team.

Frequently asked questions

Can Sales Intelligence work with a small pipeline?

Yes. Scoring produces best results with 50–100 active leads. Even with 30 leads, you gain immediate clarity. The "automated follow-ups" component works below that too: zero forgotten lead.

Do customers see they've been scored?

No. Scoring is an internal tool. Your rep sees the score, but the customer sees personalized, relevant, well-timed communication—that's all.

Does our ERP need to be "perfect"?

No, but you need the basics: customer, order dates, amounts, products. We clean raw data with fuzzy matching and deduplication. "Sales-ready" data emerges automatically.

Does the 30-second quote really work?

Yes, if your catalog is in the ERP. The rep dictates (text or voice), the agent parses with fuzzy matching on product references, assembles the quote, and sends it. For a 500+ product catalog, that's 80% automation.

How does this improve average cart size?

Three levers: (1) recommendations based on customer's equipment fleet, (2) predictive maintenance alerts (customer needs part replacement in 30 days), (3) upsell from purchase history. It's calculated automatically; your rep proposes, doesn't search.

What if customer data is incomplete?

Scoring works even at 60 percent completeness. We calculate on what you have. The more you use it, the more data fills in naturally (reps complete missing fields to improve recommendations). By month three, you typically have 90 percent completeness.
