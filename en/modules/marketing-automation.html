Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need a huge product catalog?

No. The recommendation engine works from 50 products. Smaller catalogs benefit more from segmentation and timing. We optimize message quality, not catalog size.

How many emails/month is optimal?

2024 data: 9-16 emails/month delivers the best ROI (4600%). Fewer emails mean low reach and missed opportunities; more mean subscriber fatigue and increased unsubscribes. We intelligently optimize email frequency per segment.

What about unsubscribe rates?

Smart segmentation cuts unsubscribes by 40-60%. A prospect who never bought category X doesn't get emails about it. Unsubscribes reflect only true "not interested."

How does the sales link work?

When a prospect clicks, stays 2+ minutes, or downloads, an alert fires to the rep's CRM (if you use one) or via email. Context: segment, action, products viewed. Zero timing lost.

Do compatibility matrices auto-generate?

Yes, if your ERP holds product-equipment links. We validate cross-references. Matrices export as PDF and update automatically monthly.

How do we avoid subscriber fatigue?

The system intelligently controls frequency by segment. No two emails same day to same contact. Opt-in preferences and anti-spam rules respected. From 12% bounce rate on a segment, we alert for pause or strategy revision.

Is AI-generated content good enough?

Auto-generated content starts strong but isn't perfect. We recommend: week 1, you review and edit generated templates. From there, the system learns your voice and tone. By week 4, 80% of outgoing content needs zero revision. It's a partnership, not a black box. You remain in control of quality and brand voice.
