When a generic website costs more than a real strategy

You have a website? Yes. Does it generate leads? Hardly. Here's why: it's disconnected from your business. It shows a product photo, a price, a cart. But it's not *your* business.

The African French-speaking e-commerce market will reach 40 billion USD in 2025 per industry data, growing 11.9% annually. Your competitor with a *real* ERP-integrated website captures 3-4x more leads than you do.

Where Digital Platform plugs in

Real-time catalog: Your stock, prices, descriptions come straight from your ERP. No re-entry, no lag. When a reference sells out, it vanishes instantly.

Premium design: Lighthouse score 95+, dark mode, fluid animations, fully responsive. Progressive Web App installable. Every page designed for conversion, not just existence.

Brand reinforcement: Design system, professionally branded documents (quotes/invoices in your colors), expertise blog, case studies. Every touchpoint strengthens your credibility.

The impact: from visit to order

Organic traffic: A well-integrated SEO site can generate 2-3x more traffic than a generic site after 6 months.

Conversion: When stock is *real*, when prices are *current*, when experience is *smooth*, customers buy. No overstocking surprises, no erratic requests, no delivery shocks.

Fewer support calls: Customers find answers on your site (FAQ, history, docs). Zero calls like "Do you have this?" or "What's the price?". Your sales team can focus on real problems.

Illustrative scenario: B2B materials trader

You're a building materials dealer. You serve 200 construction firms. Today:

  • You have an old, non-responsive website
  • Clients call for prices or email requests
  • Displayed stock is never current
  • No organic search visibility: nobody finds you for "cheap cement Casablanca"

With Digital Platform:

  • Week 1-2: Premium 2026 site, responsive, 3000 product catalog.
  • Week 3: Real-time stock integration. Availability updates per minute.
  • Week 4: B2B pricing. Each client sees negotiated prices, volume history.
  • Month 2-3: SEO and content. Articles on cement buying, construction guides, case studies.
  • Month 4+: Rank #1-3 for "cheap cement Morocco", "materials supplier Casablanca", etc.

Result: 80% of orders come online, zero "do you have cement?" calls, stable organic traffic, increased credibility with new clients.

Limitations and prerequisites

What takes time: SEO is never "fast". You can rank for niche queries in 3 months, but competitive keywords take 6-12 months minimum.

Prerequisites: Complete, current catalog in your ERP. Product photos help enormously (not mandatory but recommended). Commitment to editorial content (blog, articles, guides).

The reality: Digital Platform isn't a generic website. It's an investment in your sales strategy. If you can't deliver online or don't want to manage direct clients, it's not for you. Otherwise, it's a 10x vs standard web agencies.

In parallel: if your ERP doesn't expose data (no API, no database access), we can still build the platform, but integration will be partially manual. Discuss with us before starting.

Digital platform questions

Common questions about deployment, continuity, and managing a Digital Platform connected to your ERP. Everything you need to know before launch.

Can Digital Platform work with our existing design?

Either keep your design or rebuild from scratch. Most migrate to 2026 design (dark mode, animations, responsive). No technical constraints.

How many articles/products can we handle?

No limit. 100, 10,000, 100,000 products—all work. Ultra-fast search (full-text indexing), multi-criteria filters, dynamic facets.

Can customers stay anonymous or must they log in?

Your choice. Public catalog with authenticated checkout, or restricted access for B2B clients. Dynamic pricing per customer/volume. Fully flexible.

What happens to customer data from the old site?

Complete migration included. Histories, preferences, payment data—all come with you. Zero loss, guaranteed continuity.

Who handles SEO and content after launch?

You or us, your choice. Infrastructure (meta tags, schema, sitemap, performance)—us. Editorial (blog, articles, studies)—you or us.
