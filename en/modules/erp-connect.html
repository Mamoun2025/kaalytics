Access your data without touching your system

Your ERP has worked correctly for years. You know its limits, its quirks, its workflows. You don't have 2 million for a complete rebuild. And you don't need one.

ERP Connect gives you access to your data without touching it. Bidirectional read and write, connected dashboards, native modules deployed on dedicated servers. Your ERP stays intact. Its workflows remain unchanged. You just gain an intelligent layer on top.

How it works

For Odoo: Native connection via XML-RPC and direct PostgreSQL access. No external APIs, no latency, real-time data.

For SAP, Sage, Dynamics: Extensible REST architecture. We connect what your systems expose, and what they don't (direct database access when needed and secure).

For legacy systems (AS400, Access, DOS): CSV import, FTP connection, proprietary APIs, or direct file reading. No system is too old to be connected.

Deployment: Dedicated VPS with multi-tenant isolation. Each subsidiary sees only its own data. Redundancy, monitoring, 24/7 alerts.

  • Your business workflows unchanged. People continue working as before.

Illustrative scenario: distribution company

Consider a SME distributor with 3 sites and legacy Odoo. Today:

  • Sales reps send orders as PDFs
  • Someone manually enters them into Odoo
  • Ops print and handle orders on paper
  • No cross-site inventory visibility

With ERP Connect:

  • Days 1-2: Order tracking dashboard. Real-time reads from Odoo.
  • Days 5-10: Order entry interface writing to Odoo. Zero re-entry.
  • Days 15-20: Unified inventory view across sites. One-click reallocation.
  • Days 25-30: Business modules (overstocking suggestions, price alerts, past-due chasing).

Result: 10 hours administrative time saved weekly, zero double-entry, real-time visibility. And Odoo hasn't changed a line.

Limitations and prerequisites

What we can't do: Modify your ERP or its workflows. ERP Connect connects *to* you, not *into* you. If you need custom Odoo development, that's a separate engagement (we do that too).

Prerequisites: API access or database read access (credentials, data format documentation). For Odoo, PostgreSQL access is preferred (faster and more secure than XML-RPC).

For legacy systems without APIs: we connect via flat files (CSV, Excel) or FTP. Slightly slower for real-time, but it works. The trade-off: you manage extracts, we wire the rest intelligently.

Guarantees: RTO less than 5 minutes (recovery time objective). RPO less than 30 seconds (recovery point objective). External security audit available. End-to-end encryption optional.

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions from clients before getting started with ERP Connect. If yours isn't here, contact us directly via WhatsApp.

Do we need to modify our ERP to connect ERP Connect?

No. ERP Connect connects *to* your data, not *into* your system. Zero code changes, zero workflow disruption. Your ERP continues exactly as it is, without interruption. That's our core promise: innovation without disruption.

How long until production?

2-4 weeks for standard systems (Odoo), up to 8 weeks for complex ones (SAP, Dynamics). Full testing included, progressive migration by module.

What if your platform goes down?

Your ERP keeps running normally. Only external dashboards and modules will be unavailable. Your business processes are never blocked by our platform. Redundancy and automatic failover for less than 5 minutes RTO.

Cost of adding another ERP connection?

Depends on the system. Odoo to Odoo: 2-3 days. Odoo to SAP: 3-5 days. Get a quote from us—always cheaper than manual integration or standard ETL.

Is our data secure?

Yes. TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, at rest, and in logs. Your data never leaves your zone (OVH VPS, AWS, or on-premise per your choice). Third-party security audit available.
