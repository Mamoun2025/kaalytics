Prerequisites That Block

What needs to be in place

Current production data
We need to know: how much each equipment extracts daily, cycle times, stops. If you have SCADA or automation systems: perfect, we connect. If it's manual: digitalize first.
Network coverage at extraction sites
Mining and quarries: typically sparse 4G. We install industrial WiFi relays or configure batched updates (data collected locally, synced when connected). Works in all geographic conditions.
Maintenance history clarity
When was the last major overhaul? What components were replaced? Equipment manufacturer manuals must be accessible or digitalized. This is the foundation of prediction.
Equipment operator acceptance
Operators see tracking as a safety tool (anomaly alerts = fewer accidents) and planning aid (predictive maintenance = less chaos). 2 days training. Zero resistance in practice.

Key Performance Indicators

Mining and quarry KPIs

1
Mechanical availability (uptime)
Equipment availability determines real production capacity. Poorly managed fleets suffer frequent stops. With calibrated predictive maintenance, you keep assets in good shape and reduce unexpected failures. Every gain increases productive capacity.
2
Cost per ton extracted
Reactive maintenance adds significant per-ton cost (urgent interventions, specialist fees). Well-executed predictive maintenance reduces that cost by avoiding failures. High daily volumes make this impact substantial.
3
Equipment utilization vs. contracted capacity
If you're contracted to deliver 500 tons/day but only produce 400, you lose money and disappoint the client. Real-time tracking = fast correction before compounding.
4
Regulatory compliance and audit trail
Energy = 50% of quarry operating cost (2025 data). Euro/Stage V norms = mandatory. Complete GPS + maintenance history = proof of compliant operations. Zero audit surprises.

Common Objections

Frank answers

Dust and harsh conditions will destroy sensors.
GPS/RFID trackers we install are hardened for hostile environments: dust, vibration, humidity. 5-7 year lifespan with no maintenance. If a unit fails, it's under warranty.
We have no network on site.
We install industrial WiFi relays or configure batched updates (data collected locally, synchronized when connected). Zero operational impact. Maximum 2-3 hour delay possible.
Installation will halt production.
Installed during non-peak hours or on redundant equipment. A single excavator sensor takes 2-3 hours. You lose nothing and gain everything after.
Our production data isn't accurate yet.
Normal. We start with what you currently have, improving gradually with FleetOps integration. After 4-6 weeks, you'll have a true, reliable view of operations. Significantly more accurate than current manual estimates and spreadsheet-based tracking.
Audits will become a nightmare.
Opposite. Auditors want: complete equipment history, intervention traceability, norm compliance proof. FleetOps delivers exactly that. Auditors love it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers

How soon do we improve uptime?
As soon as we have 2-3 weeks of operational data. First alerts start appearing in week 4. Measurable uptime gains: months 2-3. Stable gains: months 4-6 when we have enough data for reliable patterns.
What about old equipment without sensors?
We add robust external trackers. Cost: 300-500 euros per machine. 5-year lifespan. For a 500k excavator you want to maximize, it's negligible.
Does this affect site safety?
Zero negative impact. You detect anomalies earlier (engine overheating, unusual slowdown = possible safety issues). Anomaly alerts = accident prevention.
How much training do operators need?
Two days maximum. They see anomaly alerts and maintenance scheduling. Daily work doesn't change. Training is straightforward.
What if we run multiple extraction sites?
FleetOps handles this natively: multi-site, data consolidation, central + local dashboards. See each site's productivity and the group's simultaneously.
How do we decide end-of-life for aging equipment?
FleetOps shows true TCO per machine: purchase amortized + maintenance + opportunity cost. When a unit nears contract end, you see whether upgrading pays or retiring makes sense. Data-driven choice, not guesswork.
Can we use this for subcontractor billing?
Yes, absolutely. If you lease an excavator to a contractor, FleetOps tracks precise actual use: operating hours, fuel consumption, anomalies and maintenance events. Automatic usage-based billing prevents estimation errors. End of billing disputes and customer disagreements.
