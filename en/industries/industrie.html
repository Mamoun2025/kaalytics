Prerequisites That Block

What needs to be in place

Basic production data
We need to know: what you produce, cycle times, scrap rates. If it's on paper or sticky notes, digitalize first. If it's in an MES or ERP: we connect directly.
Access to production equipment
Sensor installation requires physical access. Data reading: OPC-UA or Modbus interface, or CSV imports. Permissions and safety validation upfront.
Equipment documentation available
Machine manuals, electrical schematics, maintenance history. If everything is paper archives, we move slower. If it's organized: we deploy fast.
Production team buy-in
Operators worry about work optimization pushing them harder. Reality: we optimize bottlenecks and stops, not people. 2-3 days training. It's a tool for them, not surveillance.

Key Performance Metrics

Manufacturing KPIs that matter

1
OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)
OEE varies by industry and optimization level. Leading factories show substantially higher OEE. Every improvement gains capacity. On a significant production line, OEE gains translate to additional volume and revenue.
2
Scrap rate / Defects per Million (DPM)
High-speed lines: manual inspection misses defects. Defects that pass = customer complaints or rework. Automated inspection + predictive alerts = better detection and higher final quality.
3
Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)
Reactive maintenance: machine fails, production stops. Predictive maintenance: service before failure, machine keeps running. Result: progressive elimination of unplanned downtime (visible within months).
4
Energy consumption per unit
Old or poorly tuned equipment: abnormal consumption. Consumption alerts + tuning = lower electricity bills. Savings grow with production facility size.

Common Objections

Honest answers

We have an old factory — integration is impossible.
Opposite. Old machines have no sensors: we add them externally. Vibration, temperature, electrical consumption sensors. Cost: 500-1500 euros per machine. Worth it if you have downtime.
My ERP is a black box.
Sage, SAP, Oracle, Odoo: direct connection. Proprietary legacy: daily exports. Slower but works. We discuss feasibility at demo based on your system.
Implementation will be a nightmare.
5 weeks typically. One week per module, one week stabilization. If you have 10 production lines, we onboard them gradually. Weeks 1-2: first line live. Weeks 3-5: the rest.
Our historical data is messy.
We start fresh on day one. By month two, you have quality data. Prediction starts simple (obvious anomalies) and improves with time. You don't need perfect historical records to start gaining insight and making better decisions.
This will slow down our lines.
Zero impact. Sensors don't slow anything. The system runs alongside your production. Alerts only appear if there's a real problem — no false alarms from paranoia.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers

How soon do we see OEE improvements?
As soon as we have one week of data: you see real bottlenecks. First gains: week 4 (anomaly alerts reduce surprise stops). Stable gains: months 3-4 (enough data for reliable patterns).

Yes, 100%. You're not forced to replace your ERP or MES. Kaalytics sits alongside and enriches your data. If you change systems later, migration is straightforward.

You configure it: shift supervisors for production anomalies, maintenance teams for preventive alerts, plant manager for daily summary. No spam — every alert is actionable.

Do we need to change our operational procedures?
Minimal. Supervisors and operators receive alerts and can act immediately. No major workflow restructuring required. The system integrates seamlessly into existing production routines and daily operations.
