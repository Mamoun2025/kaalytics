Our Approach

Intelligence at the Service of Operations

Most companies have already invested in management systems. Odoo, SAP, Sage, Dynamics — they all hold the core data. The problem isn't data. The problem is what you do with it.

We build the modules you need. Those who transform data into decisions, entries into actions, reports into alerts. Without replacing what's already there. Without forcing your teams to change their habits.

Every module deploys in weeks, connects to your existing systems, and delivers measurable results from day one.

What guides us

Our purpose, our direction, our principles

Three different things, often conflated. What we do today, and for whom. Where we want it to lead. And the rules we hold ourselves to along the way, including when they cost us a sale.

Mission

Simplify the lives of entrepreneurs and their teams, one task at a time.

No entrepreneur should waste their energy on tedious tasks that technology can now handle for them. We automate routine work to give everyone back what matters most: time, clarity, and the freedom to focus their energy where it has the most value. Concretely: to make usable the data companies already hold — by connecting intelligence modules to the ERP already in place, without migration, without replacing what works.

Vision

Democratize access to artificial intelligence, for the greatest number of companies.

That applied intelligence stops being a privilege of large accounts: across the French-speaking world, a fifty-person company should run its operations with the same instruments as a large group — without a data team, without a seven-figure budget, and without depending on us to keep going.

How we work

  • ExcellenceEfficient, well-crafted work, built to genuinely function in the field, in a company's concrete day-to-day.
  • Co-creationOur clients aren't mere recipients but partners; for their specific challenges, we co-create a solution suited to their needs.
  • ListeningBusiness relationships built on trust: listen, understand, and let each client decide freely, at their own pace.

Our principles

  • Usefulness before technologyA module that changes no decision does not deserve to be deployed.
  • Add, never replaceYour ERP works. Our job is to extend it, not rebuild it.
  • Your data stays yoursOn your side, in your infrastructure, readable without going through us.
  • What cannot be measured is not promisedWe would rather claim less and prove it than the other way round.
  • You must be able to leaveStop the subscription and your ERP keeps running. A tool that locks you in has no value.

Why add rather than replace

You invested in an ERP. Your teams know it. Your data lives in it. So why replace it?

What an ERP migration costs

  • 6 to 18 months of implementation
  • EUR 100k to 500k in fees (consulting plus licences)
  • Full retraining of your teams
  • Risk of data loss and of interruptions

A connected analytics layer

  • 2 to 4 weeks of deployment
  • A cost that stays known and transparent
  • Light training: the module alone, not the whole system
  • Results visible on day one

If you stop one day

Your data stays in your ERP, untouched. The modules switch off. You keep using your ERP as before. Nothing is irreversible.
